About the Project

Student veterans bring a unique perspective to academic inquiry — shaped by service, sacrifice, and resilience. This database exists to make their work discoverable and to honor the intellectual contributions they make to their fields.

Works span disciplines and institutions. If you are a student veteran with published or in-progress academic work, we encourage you to submit it for inclusion.
